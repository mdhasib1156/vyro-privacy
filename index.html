Privacy at a glance

VYRO is designed to play media selected by the user on a Windows computer. The sections below explain expected local processing and identify areas that should be confirmed against the released app.

01Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how VYRO ("the App"), published by HT Skill Hub ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the Windows desktop application.

It applies to VYRO itself. External websites or services you choose to visit may have separate privacy policies.

02Your media files

When you open a video or audio file, VYRO needs access to the selected file to play it. Playlist functionality may read file names, locations, and media metadata to arrange and play items.

Media content is processed on your device for playback. VYRO does not require you to upload your personal media to HT Skill Hub to use ordinary local-file playback.

You control which local files and folders you open in the App.

03App settings and preferences

VYRO may store preferences on your Windows device, such as the selected light or dark theme, volume, playback settings, and window layout, so your choices can be restored when the App is reopened.

These preferences are used to operate and personalize the App. Their precise storage location and retention depend on the installed version.

04Data collection and network activity

VYRO's core local media playback is intended to work without creating an online account. This policy does not claim that every build is entirely offline or that no diagnostic information can ever be processed.

Network requests, telemetry, crash reporting, online features, or external integrations—if included in a released version—must be disclosed here before publication, including their purpose and recipients.

Publisher review required: HT Skill Hub should verify the final packaged build and its dependencies before making definitive statements about analytics, network connections, or personal-data sharing.

05Third-party software

VYRO may use third-party media playback components and application frameworks to decode and render media. Those components may be subject to their own software licenses and notices.

If a feature opens an external website or connects to an external service, that service's terms and privacy practices may apply. Any such feature should be identified in the App or in an updated version of this policy.

06Security

We aim to minimize unnecessary access to user information and encourage users to obtain VYRO from trusted distribution channels. No software can be guaranteed completely secure.

Keep Windows and VYRO updated, and open media only from sources you trust.

07Your choices

  • Choose which media files to open.
  • Change supported preferences in the App.
  • Remove VYRO through Windows settings if you no longer wish to use it.

Uninstalling the App may not automatically delete all preferences stored in your Windows user profile.

08Children's privacy

VYRO is a general-purpose media player and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise the media content children access using the App.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when VYRO's features, dependencies, or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
